About the Firm

Our Philosophy

Patient stewardship over reaction.

We believe wealth is best cared for slowly, deliberately, and on a horizon measured in decades — not quarters.

Most financial mistakes are not analytical; they are emotional. They happen in the moments when fear or enthusiasm overwhelms a plan. Our role is to build a plan sound enough to hold through those moments, and to stand beside you when they come — so that decisions are made by design rather than by impulse.

Markets reward patience and punish reaction. We do not attempt to predict the next quarter, time a top, or chase a theme. We construct portfolios that are globally diversified, cost-aware, and tax-aware, then we let time and discipline do the work that timing cannot. The unglamorous decisions — rebalancing, harvesting losses, keeping costs low, staying invested — are where durable results are actually made.

This patience extends to how we grow as a firm. We work with a deliberately focused number of relationships so that each receives genuine, partner-level attention. We would rather serve fewer families well, across the whole arc of their financial lives, than serve many shallowly.

The Name

Ktema — κτῆμα.

The Greek word ktema (κτῆμα) means a possession, an asset, something owned and kept. The historian Thucydides described his work not as a performance for the moment but as a ktema es aei — "a possession for all time," something built to hold its worth across generations.

We took that phrase as our standard. Wealth, stewarded well, is not a score to be tallied each quarter; it is something entrusted to you and, in time, passed on. Our work is to help it endure beyond a single market cycle and a single generation.

What Independence Means For You

Advice with no other agenda.

Independence and a fee-based model are not slogans — they change whose interest the advice serves.

A fiduciary standard

We are held to a fiduciary standard, which obligates us to place your interests ahead of our own. It is the foundation of every recommendation we make.

Fee-based, not commissioned

We are compensated by you, transparently, for advice — not by commissions, product sales, or sales targets. Removing those incentives removes the conflicts they create.

Independent & unaffiliated

We are not owned by a bank, fund company, or insurer, and we have no proprietary products to place. We are free to recommend what we believe is genuinely best for you.

What We Stand For

The principles behind the work.

  • Client-first, without exception

    Every recommendation begins and ends with your interest. When a choice is genuinely close, we err toward what protects you.

  • Transparency in everything

    You should always understand what we recommend, why, and exactly what it costs. No hidden incentives, no surprises.

  • Discipline over prediction

    We follow process, not forecasts. A written plan, held through cycles, outperforms reaction over time.

  • Patience as a strategy

    Compounding rewards time in the market and consistency of behavior. We are built to wait, on purpose.

  • Coordination across your life

    Investments, tax, retirement, and estate are one connected picture. We treat them as a single plan, with your CPA and attorney.

  • A relationship, not a transaction

    We serve a focused number of families so each gets genuine, partner-level attention across the whole arc of their lives.
