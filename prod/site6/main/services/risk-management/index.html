Services

Risk Management

A clear-eyed review of the protections around your plan. We help you see where you are covered, where you are exposed, and how to size protection to the risks that actually matter.

What This Includes

What this includes

A single uninsured event can undo years of careful saving. Our work here is an advisory review: we examine the coverage you already hold, identify gaps, and help you weigh whether your protection still fits your life.

Life insurance review

We review existing life policies against the income, debts, and goals your family would still need to fund — checking whether coverage, structure, and beneficiaries still match the plan you have today.

Disability income coverage review

Your earning power is often your largest asset. We look at any disability coverage you hold — through an employer or privately — to see whether it would realistically replace enough income if you could not work.

Long-term care (LTC) planning

We help you think through how an extended care need might be funded — whether through insurance, dedicated assets, or a blend — so a later-life event does not fall unplanned onto your portfolio or your family.

Liability & umbrella coverage review

As assets grow, so does exposure to a serious claim. We review your liability limits and whether an umbrella policy is sized to protect the net worth you have built, rather than left at an old default.

Property & casualty exposure review

Homes, vehicles, and other property carry their own risks. We review whether the structure and limits of your property and casualty coverage line up with what you actually own and the way you live.

Protecting the plan against shocks

Beyond individual policies, we consider how the pieces fit together — so a single setback, from an illness to a lawsuit, is far less likely to force decisions that derail your long-term goals.

Our Approach

Our approach to risk management

As a fiduciary, we begin with the risks a portfolio simply cannot absorb on its own — an early death, a disabling illness, an extended care need, or a serious liability claim. The goal is not to insure everything, but to protect the plan from the shocks that would otherwise force it off course.

We size coverage to your actual exposure rather than to a rule of thumb. That means reviewing the policies you already hold, looking for both gaps and unnecessary overlap, and being candid where coverage is more, or less, than your situation calls for. Where the right answer is uncertain, we say so.

This is advisory work. We review and coordinate; the policies themselves are placed and serviced by licensed insurance professionals, whose guidance we are glad to work alongside. Our interest is in keeping protection aligned with the plan as your life changes.
