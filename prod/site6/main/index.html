About the Firm

A possession for all time.

Ktema Wealth Management was founded on a simple fiduciary conviction: advice should be given solely in the client's interest, free of the conflicts that come with commissions and proprietary products. We are independent and fee-based, which means our counsel is aligned with your outcomes — not with a sale.

We believe wealth is best stewarded slowly and deliberately. Markets reward patience and punish reaction; our role is to keep your plan disciplined through both, so that decisions are made by design rather than by emotion.

“κτῆμα ἐς αἰεί” — a possession for all time. The Greek ktema (κτῆμα) means a possession or asset. Thucydides described his history as ktema es aei — something of lasting worth. We took the name as our standard: wealth built to last beyond a single market, a single generation.

Our work is unhurried and exacting. We measure success not in a quarter, but across the arc of a family's life.
